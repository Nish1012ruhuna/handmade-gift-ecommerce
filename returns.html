Returns & cancellations

If something is not right with your order, please contact us so we can review the situation.

Report an issue

Please email support@giftora.lk with your order number, a description of the issue and clear photos if an item arrived damaged or incorrect.

Personalized and handmade products

Because some items are made or customized for a specific customer, returns and cancellations may depend on the product's preparation stage and the reason for the request. Contact us as soon as possible; a cancellation may not be possible once production or dispatch has begun.

Refunds

Any approved refund will be discussed with you and handled using an appropriate method based on the payment and order status. This page describes the support process and does not guarantee a refund in every case.

For the quickest help, contact us promptly after noticing a problem. Do not share passwords or full card details.
