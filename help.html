Frequently asked questions

Can I personalize a gift?

Some products support personalization. Check the product details and customization options before adding the item to your cart.

How can I check my order?

Open Track Order and sign in with the same account used at checkout. Only your own orders are shown.

How much is delivery?

GIFTORA advertises free delivery on orders over Rs. 2,000. Confirm any delivery fee shown in your checkout summary before placing an order.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Contact support as soon as possible. Handmade and personalized items may need special handling; see our returns policy.
