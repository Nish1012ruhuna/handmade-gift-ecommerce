Handmade Gift E-Commerce Website 

The proposed business is an online store that specializes in handmade and personalized gifts. The main concept of the business is to provide customers with unique gifts that can be purchased for different personal and social occasions.
Many customers prefer gifts that have a personal meaning rather than standard mass produced products. Personalized products allow customers to include names, photographs, messages or other details that make a gift more meaningful to the recipient. Therefore, the proposed business will combine handmade products with personalization options to provide customers with a more customized shopping experience.
The business will operate primarily through the proposed e-commerce website. Customers will be able to access the website using a web browser, browse available products and place orders online.
The website will also help the business owner manage products and customer orders through an administrative interface.
