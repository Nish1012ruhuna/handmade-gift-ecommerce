YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

This page explains, in plain language, how information may be used when you shop with GIFTORA.

Information you provide

When you register or place an order, the site may collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order information.

How information is used

Information is used to manage your account, process orders, arrange delivery, respond to support requests and maintain the website.

Payments

Payment processing may involve third-party payment services. Do not send card details or passwords to GIFTORA support by email.

Security and access

Keep your login credentials private and log out on shared devices. The website uses account sessions to restrict access to account and order information.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact support@giftora.lk.

This is a starter policy page for this project. Review and adapt it to the actual data practices and legal requirements before deploying the website publicly.
