DELIVERY INFORMATION

Shipping & delivery

We want your gift to arrive safely and be ready for its special moment.

Delivery fees

GIFTORA advertises free delivery on orders over Rs. 2,000. Any delivery fee applicable to your specific order should be displayed during checkout; please review the order total before confirming.

Processing and delivery time

Preparation time may vary depending on product availability and personalization. Customized cakes, engraved items and handmade products may require extra preparation. If you have a date-sensitive gift, contact support before placing your order.

Delivery address

Enter a complete address and a reachable phone number at checkout. Contact support promptly if you notice an address mistake after placing an order.

Order updates

Sign in and visit Track Order or My Account to review the status recorded for your order.
